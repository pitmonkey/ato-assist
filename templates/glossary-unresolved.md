# Unresolved terms

Acronyms and terms found in ingested documents that neither the baseline glossary nor `glossary.md` defines. Added automatically by `/ato-ingest`; resolved one at a time by `/ato-glossary`.

Nothing here blocks work. An item that depends on an unresolved term is marked `needs-clarification` and carries on.
